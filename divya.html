Concert questions

Sacred Music for Sacred Forests · with Go Dharmic, for the Sundarbans tigers · 19 Oct · for Divya

Start here

  1. Ask the Everyone questions to every person, in the same words.
  2. 20–30 seconds a person. Then thank them and move on.
  3. Ask them to answer in a full sentence: "I came tonight because…"
  4. Before you film: "Can we film you? It is for a documentary."
  5. After: "Can we use this in the film?" Get the yes on camera.
  6. No questions while the music plays.
  7. Tap a question when you ask it. It turns green.

Everyone

Volunteers, Hanuman Dass, audience. Same words each time, so the answers cut together.

What brought you here tonight?
आज आप यहाँ क्यों आए?
What does chanting have to do with a tiger?
कीर्तन का बाघ से क्या रिश्ता है?
The most important one. Any answer is good, even "I don't know".
Do you know who Nina Rao is?
क्या आप जानते हैं कि नीना राव कौन हैं?
If they say no, that is a good answer too. Never make anyone feel bad.

Audience · before

In the queue or at the door, before it starts.

Do you know where tonight's money goes?
क्या आप जानते हैं कि आज का पैसा कहाँ जाता है?
Have you been to a kirtan before?
क्या आप पहले कभी कीर्तन में गए हैं?

Audience · after

At the exit. People are open now. Go fast, the crowd leaves quickly.

What will you remember from tonight?
आज की शाम की कौन-सी बात आपको याद रहेगी?
How do you feel right now, in one word?
अभी आप कैसा महसूस कर रहे हैं, एक शब्द में?
Would you come if it was only a fundraiser, with no music?
अगर यह सिर्फ़ चंदे का कार्यक्रम होता, बिना संगीत के, तो क्या आप आते?

Go Dharmic volunteers

Ask the Everyone questions first. Then these.

What is your job here today?
Film them while they do it, if you can.
Why do you give your time to this?
What will tonight's money do in the Sundarbans?
Ask for one real thing it pays for. Never ask for a number.
Who did the most work today that the audience will never see?
Do not say Nina's name. Let them answer.

Hanuman Dass

Founder of Go Dharmic. Ask the Everyone questions first. A quiet corner, 5–10 minutes.

How did you and Nina start working together?
Why the Sundarbans? What will tonight pay for there?
One real thing. If he gives a number, keep it. Never guess one.
What does Nina do that the audience never sees?
Ask for one moment: "Can you remember a time when…?"
How does Nina react when the attention turns to her?
"We give too much credit to the person we can see on stage." Agree or disagree?
Read the line, then stop talking. Let him answer.

How to film

  • Hold the phone or camera horizontal.
  • Mic close to the mouth. Find a quieter corner, away from the speakers.
  • Their face to the light. The crowd or the stage behind them.
  • They look at you, not at the lens. Stand just next to the camera.
  • After each person, write first name + city in your notes app.
  • Never promise anyone they will be in the film.
  • No questions while the music plays. Film the faces instead, if the organiser says yes.
  • If someone says no, smile, thank them, move on.
